LinkedIn Post Body Copy

Shipped a new storefront for Adina Household. A premium Pakistani home goods brand — ceramics, tableware, drinkware. Products that deserve a store that feels as good as they look. Built headless with Shopify Hydrogen 2025 + React Router 7 + shadcn/ui. Full e-commerce: collections, product pages, cart, checkout, customer accounts, search. Zero Liquid templates. Deployed to Shopify Oxygen. The brief was simple: move off a generic Shopify theme onto a modern stack — without losing any commerce functionality.
